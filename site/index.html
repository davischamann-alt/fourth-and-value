How to read a game card
  • Line is the betting spread from ESPN. The small gray number is where it opened, so you can see how it moved.
  • Model is the predicted final margin. Pick is the side the model thinks covers the line.
  • Edge is how many points the model disagrees with the line by. It's labeled like a fourth-down call: Go for it is 3+ points, Lean is 1.5–3, and Punt is less, meaning no bet.
  • Cover chance is the model's chance the pick covers. During a game it updates with the score and clock. The yellow tick on the bar marks 52.4%, the break-even point at standard -110 odds.
  • The Track button saves a pick to My picks, where it's graded automatically when the game ends.
  • Tap a game for the full breakdown: what every projection says, how the line has moved, the matchup, weather, and how the game played out.

FPI is ESPN's rating: points better or worse than an average FBS team on a neutral field. Offense and Defense are points scored and allowed per game, adjusted for opponent strength. Net is offense minus defense. Click a column to sort.

How this was tested

Every FBS game from week 3 on was predicted using only information available before that week, then graded against the line. At standard -110 odds you need 52.4% to break even. Line moved our way is how often the line moved toward the pick between the open and kickoff, which is the standard sign of a real edge. Market-anchored learns its weights from earlier seasons only, so its first season isn't graded. This is analysis, not betting advice.
